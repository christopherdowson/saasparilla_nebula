# Changelog

## 1.0.0, 22 October 2026

First release.

- Saasparilla skin for Nebula 1.0: the token set in light and dark, on the platform-admin paper.
- The design beyond the tokens: Archivo, wide uppercase labels, display figures, ink-ruled cells, keys with a pressed edge, status squares, numbered navigation, flyouts on ink, square corners throughout.
- Child theme `MFRG/saasparilla` with the dashboard sales chart drawn by Saasparilla's SVG line chart.
- Dashboard instruments: revenue with a twelve-month trend, orders, new customers and average order value against last month, revenue share by payment method, unshipped orders, and the calendar of days at an orders target. Targets under Stores › Configuration › MFRG › Dashboard.
- Activation on install, with the revert on uninstall.
- Checked with axe-core and a computed-style census across the dashboard, grids, forms, order view, configuration and sign-in, in both modes.
